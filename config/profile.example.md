# Profile

## Who I am

I am an invented logistics planner in the invented town of Parcelmere. I have
six years of experience planning deliveries for an invented goods cooperative.
I compare orders, vehicle capacity and delivery windows. I use spreadsheets to
check routes and explain changes to warehouse staff. I completed vocational
training in transport planning. I do not hold a heavy vehicle licence.

I prepare a daily plan for a small fleet. When a delivery fails, I record the
cause and adjust the next plan. I have also trained new planners to use our
order system. I have not managed hiring, budgets or staff performance reviews.

## What I want next

I want a planning role where I can improve routes as well as handle daily
changes. I want time to compare several weeks of delivery records. I would
consider stock planning if the employer provides training. I want to remain a
specialist rather than become a people manager.

## What makes a job exceptional for me

A job would be exceptional if it combines delivery planning with measured
improvements in waste and missed deliveries. I want the ad to describe the
planning team, the tools and the authority to change a process. A clear
training plan for unfamiliar software would support a strong match. A job
that only enters orders would not be exceptional.

## What I do not want

I do not want sales targets or a job that mainly involves driving. I want to
avoid a role where every planning decision needs several layers of approval.
These are preferences unless I state a strict limit below.

## Hard limits

- STRICT: I cannot work overnight shifts.
- STRICT: I cannot accept a role that requires a heavy vehicle licence on the first day.

## Preferences

- PREFER: I want predictable daytime hours, with occasional planned changes.
- PREFER: I want an employer that explains how delivery problems are recorded.
- PREFER: I want training before I take responsibility for unfamiliar systems.

## Examples

Yes: a route planner who reviews failed deliveries and tests changes with a
warehouse team. The duties use my experience and add the improvement work I want.

No: an overnight dispatcher who must also drive large vehicles. Both stated
requirements conflict with my strict limits.

## Notes for the reviewer

I have not stated a minimum salary. Do not infer one. If shift times are
missing, keep that limit unknown. Treat employer claims about teamwork as
claims, not proof of working conditions. Do not assume I can meet a licence
requirement after training unless the ad explicitly permits it.
